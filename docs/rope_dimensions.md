# Rope Splicing & Cutting Dimensions

This guide outlines the specifications, calculated dimensions, and cutting instructions for the lines and ropes used in ThePerfectKiteBar. These calculations are optimized for a **65 cm bar throw** setup.

---

## 1. System Parameters & Constants

These inputs determine the geometry of the bar throw, trim travel, and splicing tolerances:

| Parameter | Value | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| **Desired Throw** | 65 cm | Slicing travel distance for the bar. |
| **Depower Move** | 25 cm | Physical travel of the trim cleat adjustor. |
| **Depower Ratio** | 1/2 | Pulley ratio for the depower line. |
| **Depower Line Tail** | 9 cm | Pull handle tail length. |
| **Sheeting/Depower Line** | 4.0 mm | Diameter of the main sheeting line. |
| **Leader Line Diameter** | 3.0 mm | Diameter of the steering leader line inside bar ends. |
| **Pig Tail / Connector Rope** | 2.5 mm | Diameter of Dyneema used for pigtails. |
| **Bungee Line Diameter** | 2.5 mm | Core elastic bungee line diameter. |
| **Splicing Bury Ratio** | 50 diameters | Standard bury depth (e.g., 12.5 cm bury for 2.5mm rope). |

---

## 2. Calculated Rope & Line Dimensions

Below are the calculated lengths and recommended cut targets for preparing each component.

> [!TIP]
> Always cut slightly longer than the target length to allow for splicing shrinkage and fine-tuning. Excess line can be trimmed after locking the knots.

| Line Component | Calculated Length | Suggested Cut Target | Splicing & Build Notes |
|----------------|-------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| **Sheeting (Depower) Line** | 195.5 cm (6.41 ft) | **185 cm** | Main 4.0mm sheeting line. Use 185 cm, slice loop, and trim excess after final assembly. |
| **Power Leader Line** | 230.5 cm (7.56 ft) | **230.5 cm** | Main power lines from cleat to V-Splitter. |
| **Leader Lines** | 174.0 cm (5.71 ft) | **180 cm** | Steering lines inside bar ends. |
| **Chafe Sleeve (ID 4-6mm)**| 88.2 cm (2.89 ft) | **96 - 97 cm** | Protects the sheeting line. *Rule of thumb:* 92cm = 63cm throw, 94cm = 65cm throw. Cut 96-97cm (38") and bury the tails in the cleat. |
| **Bungee Line** (Inside bar ends) | 242.0 cm (7.94 ft) | **150 cm (5 ft)** | 2.5mm line with a buried **32.5 cm** bungee elastic core inside. |

---

## 3. Pigtail Configurations

Pigtails act as modular connectors between your flying lines and the kite's bridles. They are consumable parts and should be checked regularly for wear.

| Specification | Steering Line Pigtail (Loop-to-Loop) | Power Line Pigtail (Adjustable Loop-to-Knot) |
|---------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Rope Diameter** | 2.5 mm Dyneema | 2.5 mm Dyneema |
| **Bury Ratio** | 50x diameter (12.5 cm bury) | 50x diameter (12.5 cm bury) |
| **Finished Length** | 40 cm | 40 cm |
| **Loop Length** | 3 cm (Total splice: 10-12 cm) | 4 cm |
| **Adjustment Detail**| 4 cm loop knot | 20 cm knot-adjustment tail |
| **Splicing Shrinkage**| 20% | 20% |
| **Calculated Target** | 77 cm (2.53 ft) | 94 cm (3.08 ft) |
| **Recommended Cut** | **100 cm (3 ft)** | **124 cm (4 ft)** |

---

## 4. Bungee Elastic Details

These elastic sections keep steering lines tensioned when sheeted out and keep the safety line compact.

* **Bungee Core (Other Ends)**:
  * For **65mm Bar End** (OD 24mm): **30 cm** bungee length.
  * For **100mm Bar End** (OD 24mm): **37 cm** bungee length.
  * *Bungee Tail (after final knot)*: **5 cm** excess.
* **Quick Release (QR)** (With 4mm bolts and spacing for knots):
  * **35 cm** cut length (typically **27 cm** is used after cutting excess).
* **Polyurethane (PU) Tube Length** (For 24mm bar protection): **100 mm (10 cm)**.

---

## 5. Camera Spacing from V-Splitter

If you are using a line mount for an action camera, position it at the following distances above the V-splitter to keep the lines out of the frame:

* **Insta360 / 360 Cameras**: **20 cm (8 inches)** above the splitter.
* **GoPro / Standard Action Cameras**: **61 cm (2 feet)** above the splitter.
